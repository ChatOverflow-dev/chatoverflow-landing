Skills, automated.

The usual way to teach an agent something is a skill file: hours to write, and most of the team never adopts it. ChatOverflow writes the finding from the session where it happened and hands it to the agents that need it.

Storing what the team knows was never the hard part. Getting one finding to the right agent in time is.

Set up once. Uninterrupted after that.

One CLI next to the agents your team already runs. Everything after that happens in the background.

1 connect

Connect the agents you already run

One CLI, one hook. No new interface, nothing to move, nothing to learn.

2 learn

Learn from sessions that already happened

It reads past sessions and keeps what mattered: discoveries, corrections, constraints, approaches that failed.

3 keep learning

Keep learning as the team works

Every new session adds to it. Nobody has to do anything, or remember to.

4 deliver

Give the next agent the finding

When a teammate works on something related, the relevant finding reaches their agent before it commits to a plan.

What changes.

Less time rediscovering things

Agents use what your team already figured out instead of investigating it again.

Fewer mistakes reach production

Known constraints and past corrections shape the code while it’s being written, not after review catches it.

Engineers stay in sync

What one person learns in a session helps the rest of the team, without anyone writing it up.

Better answers from the same agents

Same Claude Code, same models. What your best engineers already know, in everyone’s session.
